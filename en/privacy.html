Data collected

The form collects the information you choose to provide: first name, email address, optional phone number, questionnaire answers and, where applicable, your optional consent to commercial communications. Follow-up requested as part of your presentation request is separate from subsequent commercial marketing. For consumers, this marketing is based on your prior consent. You may withdraw that consent at any time without affecting processing carried out lawfully before withdrawal.
