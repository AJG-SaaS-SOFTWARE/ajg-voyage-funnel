Data collected

The form collects the information you choose to provide: first name, email address, optional phone number, questionnaire answers and, where applicable, your optional consent to commercial communications. It also records the language used, an internal lead score, UTM parameters present in the page URL, the landing-page URL and the referring URL supplied by the browser.

UTM parameters, landing URL and referring URL are used to understand where requests originate and the effectiveness of communication channels. The internal score is calculated from questionnaire answers to organise and personalise follow-up. It does not make any automated decision that has legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Purposes and legal bases

Information needed for your request is used to reply to you, follow up specifically on that request and, if you wish, organise a presentation. This processing is based on the legitimate interest in responding to requests voluntarily submitted through the website, in line with the reasonable expectations of the person making the request.

Follow-up requested as part of your presentation request is separate from subsequent commercial marketing. If you separately tick the optional commercial communications box, your contact details may be used to send occasional commercial information by email and, if you provided your phone number, by SMS or phone. For consumers, this marketing is based on your prior consent. You may withdraw that consent at any time without affecting processing carried out lawfully before withdrawal.

Technical data strictly necessary for operation, security and prevention of abuse may also be processed on the basis of the legitimate interest in keeping the website secure and functional.

Recipients and service providers

Data submitted through the form is processed by the technical providers required to operate the service: Vercel, Inc. for website hosting and execution of the form API, Supabase for storage of requests and internal journey statistics, and Resend for sending Alexandre Gallet a technical notification containing the information needed to follow up on a new request. If you choose to book a meeting, you are redirected to Calendly, an external scheduling service. Information you enter on Calendly is then processed by Calendly and the meeting organiser under the terms and privacy notices applicable to that service.

AJG Voyage does not embed Calendly within its own pages: the service opens only when you choose to follow a booking link.

Some illustration images are still loaded from images.unsplash.com. Until their migration to local hosting is complete, loading those images creates a technical request to Unsplash that may transmit information including the IP address and browser details.

International transfers

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Vercel states that its primary processing facilities are in the United States and its Data Processing Addendum provides the mechanisms applicable to international transfers, including the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and, where required, the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. Supabase also provides lawful transfer mechanisms, including Standard Contractual Clauses where required. Resend stores customer data in the United States and safeguards transfers from the EEA through mechanisms including Standard Contractual Clauses and its participation in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Calendly applies the safeguards described in its current contractual and privacy documentation.

Retention period

Prospect data is retained for a maximum of three years from collection or from the last contact initiated by the prospect. Data used for marketing is no longer used for that purpose after consent is withdrawn. An erasure request may result in earlier deletion unless a legal obligation requires a different retention period.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction or erasure of your data, or restriction of its processing. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw your consent to commercial communications at any time. The right to data portability applies when the conditions set out in the GDPR are met.

To exercise your rights or ask a question about your personal data: gallet08@gmail.com.

If, after contacting us, you believe your rights have not been respected, you may lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

Cookies, local storage and external services

AJG Voyage does not currently install advertising tools or third-party analytics services on its own pages. The site uses functional local storage named ajg_language_preference when you voluntarily choose a language. It remembers that choice on your device so that the expected language version can be shown on future visits. The site also measures, for its own purposes, certain aggregate journey steps: questionnaire start and completion, request submission and click through to Calendly. These events are stored in Supabase together with language, page path and, when present, UTM source, medium and campaign parameters. This measurement no longer uses a session identifier stored on your device, and the measurement table contains no name, email address or phone number. These data are not used for targeted advertising or cross-site tracking and are automatically deleted after 13 months.

Booking links open Calendly on an external website. From that point, Calendly applies its own privacy notice and its own rules regarding cookies and similar technologies.

Updates

This notice may be updated, in particular when the activity is formally registered, new tools are added or data-processing practices change.
