Who is introducing Travel Advantage?

Travel first. Referral second.

Hi, I’m Alexandre. I’ve lived in both France and Wales, including 8 years in Wales. Living between two countries and two cultures is a big part of what gave me the desire to travel, discover new places and stay curious about the world.

I first discovered Travel Advantage as a customer, for the travel side and the benefits it offers. That is what interested me first. The referral side came later, once I felt comfortable sharing the concept with other people.

Today, my aim is simply to show you how it works, what I find useful, the points worth checking and to answer your questions. Then you can decide at your own pace whether it feels relevant to you.

Frequently asked questions

A few useful points before you continue.

What happens after the questionnaire?

You can access a free presentation to see how the concept works and ask your questions. If you later decide to explore membership, the available plans are presented separately with their official terms and pricing.

How is the business side presented?

The presentation explains the referral model and how the independent activity works in practical terms. As with any independent activity, results vary according to experience, effort, context and many individual factors.

How are my details used?

Your details are used to handle your request, organise presentation follow-up and send commercial information only when you have chosen to receive it. The website collects the information useful for that exchange.
