Is it for you?

Before going further: could this concept genuinely fit the way you travel?

Travel Advantage is not meant to be for everyone. A few simple points can already tell you whether it is worth exploring.

It may be worth a look if…
  • you travel several times a year;
  • you already compare options before booking;
  • you travel as a couple, with family or friends;
  • you are open to a membership-based model;
  • you want to understand the conditions before deciding;
  • you like the idea of exploring before committing.
It may be less relevant if…
  • you travel very rarely;
  • you do not want any type of membership;
  • you expect guaranteed savings on every booking;
  • you are looking for guaranteed income or results;
  • you do not want to compare different options;
  • you are looking for a miracle solution rather than a service to evaluate.
